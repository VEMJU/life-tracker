Is it the app, or the machine?

Three tests, each adding one thing. This page is nearly empty, so if the FIRST test is already slow, nothing in your app is to blame — the browser cannot paint on this machine, and the fix is a browser or driver setting rather than any amount of code. Run them in order.

1 · An empty page

No canvas, no blur, no animation. This is the floor — the best your browser can do.

2 · Plus a full-screen animated canvas

What bg.js does: 400 shapes redrawn every frame across the whole window.

3 · Plus 20 backdrop-filter panels

What the cards do: twenty elements each blurring whatever is behind them.

Run all three, then this will tell you what it means.
